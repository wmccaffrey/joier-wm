The Atelier

Made for the Person You Love Most.

Every commission I take starts the same way: I ask what this piece is for. Not which metal or what budget — those come later. I need to know who this is for, what it means, and what it should feel like to wear. That conversation is where the piece actually begins.

I design everything from scratch, in Manila, and I work with one client at a time. When you commission from JOIER, every decision — form, stone, finish, weight — comes through me.

Private Commissions

Begin Your
Commission

Every piece begins here, with a few details and one honest conversation.

For ₱3,000 — credited in full toward your finished piece — I design your jewelry and send you detailed CAD studies: the proposed form, the materials, and the finished look, before anything is cast or carved. A production quote is included.

When the design feels right, two paths are open. Commission the piece and the ₱3,000 is credited toward your final price. Or keep the design package as-is — the renders, materials study, and production notes are yours, with no further obligation.
