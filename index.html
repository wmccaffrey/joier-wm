Private Commissions

Begin Your
Commission

Every JOIER piece begins with a conversation. Share your vision, or simply share your instinct, and Walter will develop a design study tailored to you.

Commissions typically begin at ₱35,000, with most pieces in gold starting higher. Each brief is reviewed personally, with a response within 24–48 hours. First design direction is typically ready 2–3 weeks after approval. No commitment required until you're confident in the direction.
